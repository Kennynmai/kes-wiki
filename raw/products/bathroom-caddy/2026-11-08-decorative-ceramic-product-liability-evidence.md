## 1. 决定性依据：21 CFR §109.16【核】

来源：eCFR 官方 <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-109/section-109.16>（本轮 WebFetch 逐字取回）。授权法条列 21 U.S.C. 321 / 336 / **342** / 346 / 346a / 348 / 371，**不含 343**（即立法定位为 adulteration 而非 misbranding）。源 42 FR 52819 (1977-09-30)，修订 59 FR 1641 (1994-01-12)。

### 1.1 (a) 立法理由（原文）

> "Lead is a toxic metal that is used as a component of glazes and decorative decals on ceramics, including some ornamental and decorative ceramicware. The use of ornamental or decorative ceramicware to prepare, serve, or hold food may result in the leaching of lead from the glaze or decoration into the food. The provisions of paragraph (b) of this section are necessary to ensure that ornamental or decorative ceramicware bear adequate indications that they are not to be used for food-handling purposes."

**注意 FDA 直接点名 "decorative decals"（装饰花纸）**，且立法理由是「消费者无法自行识别，故须器物本体自带充分指示」。

### 1.2 (b) 法定推定（原文，核心）

> "Ornamental or decorative ceramicware initially introduced or initially delivered for introduction into interstate commerce on or after July 13, 1994 **appears to be suitable for food use will be considered to be for food use unless**:"

即：**只要「看起来适合装食物」，法律上就当作食品用陶瓷**，须符合铅镉溶出行动水平；想脱身只有两条法定出口。

**出口 1 — (b)(1) 贴标 + 底部永久语句，两者必须同时具备：**

> "(i) A conspicuous stick-on label on a surface clearly visible to consumers that states in legible script in letters at least **3.2 millimeters (0.125 inch)** in height **one of the following messages**: 'Not for Food Use. May Poison Food,' 'Not for Food Use. Glaze contains lead. Food Use May Result in Lead Poisoning,' and 'Not for Food Use—Food Consumed from this Vessel May be Harmful,' and"

> "(ii) A conspicuous and legible **permanent statement** of the message selected from paragraph (b)(1)(i) of this section **molded or fired onto the exterior surface of the base** or, when the ceramicware is not fired after decoration, permanently painted onto the exterior surface of the base. This permanent statement shall be in letters at least 3.2 millimeters (0.125 inch) in height, except that if insufficient space exists ... provided that the letters are at least **1.6 millimeters (0.062 inch)** in height; or"

三句法定措辞逐字为：
1. `Not for Food Use. May Poison Food`
2. `Not for Food Use. Glaze contains lead. Food Use May Result in Lead Poisoning`
3. `Not for Food Use—Food Consumed from this Vessel May be Harmful`

**出口 2 — (b)(2)：**

> "A hole is bored through the potential food-contact surface."

### 1.3 (c) 「Decorative」字样的法律地位（原文）

> "(1) A further explanatory statement concerning the decorative nature of the piece, such as 'Decorative' or 'For Decorative Purposes Only,' may be used; **however, such additional statement shall be placed after the required statement**."

> "(2) A symbol may be used to advise that a piece of ornamental or decorative ceramicware is not to be used with food, as illustrated below. The circle of the above symbol should be at least **2.54 centimeters (1 inch)** in diameter. The symbol may be used on the temporary label or applied to the base of the piece in the same manner as the permanent statement."

### 1.4 对本命题的直接推论

`Food Safe: No`、`Not for direct food contact`、`Decorative use only` **均不是** (b)(1)(i) 三句法定措辞之一，也未满足 (b)(1)(ii) 底部永久模压/烧制，更未钻孔。而 (c)(1) 把 `Decorative` / `For Decorative Purposes Only` 明确定位为**只能置于法定语句之后的可选附加语**。⇒ 这些自创措辞**不构成豁免，反而构成新的独立合规缺陷**：产品仍被法定视为食品用陶瓷，须过 FDA 铅镉行动水平；同时措辞本身可能被认定为误导性标注。

## 2. 美国产品责任法：免责声明挡不住什么【原 / 子任务检索】

### 2.1 条文编号纠正（重要，避免引用错误）

| 常见误引 | 正确（Restatement (Third) of Torts: Products Liability） |
|---|---|
| 「§16 = 可预见误用」 | **§16 = Increased harm due to product defect**（Jahn v. Hyundai 逐字引述）[8] |
| 「§18 = 警示缺陷」 | **§18 = "Disclaimers, Limitations, Waivers, and Other Contractual Exculpations as Defenses to Products Liability Claims for Harm to Persons"** [1][2][7] |
| — | 警示缺陷在 **§2(c)**；「警示不能替代合理安全设计」在 **§2 cmt l**；营销形成的消费者期待在 **§2 cmt f**；可预见误用/改动在 **§2 cmt p、§15 cmt b、§17 cmt c** [3][4][18] |

§18 的**标题本身就是本案答案**：整节专论「免责声明能否作为人身伤害产品责任索赔的抗辩」。

### 2.2 §18 原文与判例

> "Disclaimers and limitations of remedies by product sellers or other distributors, waivers by product purchasers, and other similar contractual exculpations, **oral or written, do not bar or reduce otherwise valid products liability claims** against sellers or other distributors of new products **for harm to persons**" [7]

- **Collins v. Uniroyal, Inc.**, 64 N.J. 260, 315 A.2d 30 (1974)：严格责任之诉中 "any disclaimer or limitation of remedy or liability would be without effect"，援引 Santor v. A & M Karagheusian, 44 N.J. 52 (1965) [12][13]
- **Henningsen v. Bloomfield Motors**, 32 N.J. 358, 161 A.2d 69 (1960)：默示担保免责违反公共政策 [14]
- **Uniroyal Goodrich Tire Co. v. Martinez**, 977 S.W.2d 328, 336–37 (Tex. 1998)：明确拒绝 §402A cmt j 的「充分警示即无缺陷」路径；§2 cmt l "Warnings are not … a substitute for the provision of a reasonably safe design" [4][5]
- **Uloth v. City Tank Corp.**, 376 Mass. 874, 384 N.E.2d 1188, 1192 (1978)；**Sturm, Ruger & Co. v. Day**, 594 P.2d 38, 44 (Alaska 1979) 同向 [6]
- §402A cmt h：产品须对「正常处理/食用」安全；**卖方有理由预见某一特定用途会致害时须充分警示，未警示即构成缺陷** [9]
- §402A cmt k（unavoidably unsafe products）限于药品疫苗且以 properly prepared + proper directions and warning 为前提，**装饰陶瓷不适用** [10]
- 州差异：对人身伤害几乎一致否定免责（§18 为压倒性规则）；例外仅及纯经济损失与具充分议价力的商业买方（§18 cmt c/d）[7][15]。设计缺陷判准分歧（consumer expectations vs RAD；佛州最高法院 No. SC12-2075 拒绝采纳 §2(b)）影响举证而非免责效力 [62]

### 2.3 可预见误用 / 表征矛盾

- **Liriano v. Hobart Corp.**, 92 N.Y.2d 232, 700 N.E.2d 303 (N.Y. 1998)：对可预见的改动/误用仍负警示义务，陪审团按 5% / 95% / 33⅓% 分摊 [20]
- **Eskridge v. Pacific Cycle, Inc.**, 556 F. App'x 182 (4th Cir. 2014)：可预见的错误安装下警示不能替代设计 [6]
- Louisiana R.S. §9:2800.53 定义 "reasonably anticipated use" 为「制造商应合理预期普通人在相同或类似情境下的使用」；§17 cmt c：误用不是独立抗辩，只进入缺陷、因果、比较责任三层 [18][21][22]
- 本场景：外形为盆/盘 + 命名 `cupcake stand` + 甜点架类目 + 主图放纸杯蛋糕 + A+/社媒 ⇒ 盛放食物**根本不是误用**，而是 §2 cmt f 所称 "expectations arising from product portrayal and marketing" 制造的期待 [4][9]
- **UCC §2-313(1)(b)(c)**：description / sample or model 构成明示担保，无需 "warrant" 字样 [23]；**§2-316(1)**：与明示担保相矛盾的否定/限制条款 "**inoperative to the extent that such construction is unreasonable**" [24]；**§2-719(3)**：consumer goods 之人身伤害后果损害赔偿限制 "**prima facie unconscionable**" [25]
- **FTC Act §5**, 15 U.S.C. §45(a)(1)：unfair or deceptive acts 违法；采 "**overall net impression**" 标准，且 "fine print disclosures … are unlikely to cure an otherwise deceptive representation" [26][28]。§5 **无私人诉权**（Alfred Dunhill Ltd. v. Interstate Cigar Co., 499 F.2d 232, 237 (2d Cir. 1974)），消费者走各州 UDAP [30]
- **Lanham Act §43(a)(1)(B)**, 15 U.S.C. §1125(a)(1)(B)：商业广告中 misrepresents the nature, characteristics, qualities 者负民事责任；Lexmark Int'l v. Static Control, 572 U.S. 118, 131–32 (2014) 须商业声誉或销售受损，消费者无 standing [27][30]

### 2.4 警示有效性要件 / open and obvious / 减责

- §2 cmt i：内容可理解性、表达强度、预期使用者特征；cmt j：无义务警示 obvious or generally known 风险 [17]
- 有效要件：显著（对比色/字号/位置）、销售时点或使用前、到达最终购买者、语言、持久、**对已知风险具体**（须点名铅及健康后果）。21 CFR 109.16 与 27 CCR §25603 提供具体基准 [31][42]
- 铅溶出**非**消费者显而易见危险：§109.16(a)、FDA Q&A「铅无法洗/煮去除、须看标签」[31][35]；CT DPH 2021-06-14 通报两儿童及一家长因陶瓷罐铅中毒并启动 embargo/recall [36]
- §17(a)(b)：原告过错仅**减责**不免责；纯比较 vs 修正比较（>50% 归零）因州而异；§402A cmt n 仅「明知危险而自愿不合理使用」之 assumption of risk 为完全抗辩，Third Restatement 未沿用；**General Motors Corp. v. Sanchez**, 997 S.W.2d 584 (Tex. 1999) 明言消费者无义务发现/防备缺陷 [18][19]

### 2.5 责任链与中国卖家

- §402A cmt b / §1：商业链上任一卖方均可被告——制造商、**importer of record**、品牌方/表见制造商（Third Restatement §14）、分销商、零售商、平台
- **Bolger v. Amazon.com, LLC**, 53 Cal. App. 5th 431 (2020)：FBA 下 Amazon 属分销链 "integral part"，可负严格责任；**State Farm Fire & Cas. Co. v. Amazon**, 390 F. Supp. 3d 964 (W.D. Wis. 2019)：Amazon 是中国卖家与美国市场的「唯一通道」[51][52]
- 分歧：**Erie Ins. Co. v. Amazon**, 925 F.3d 135 (4th Cir. 2019)、**Amazon.com v. McMillan**, 625 S.W.3d 101 (Tex. 2021) 不认；**Oberdorf v. Amazon**, 930 F.3d 136 (3d Cir. 2019) **已被全席复议撤销、2020 年和解，不得作为判例援引** [52][53]
- 送达：**Water Splash, Inc. v. Menon**, 581 U.S. 271 (2017)（Hague Service Convention 专属）；**Smart Study Co. v. Acuteye-US**, 620 F. Supp. 3d 1382 (S.D.N.Y. 2022)、**Kangol LLC v. Hangzhou Chuanyue Silk**, 177 F.4th 793 (7th Cir. 2026)、**Smart Study Co. v. Shenzhenshixindajixieyouxiangongsi**, 164 F.4th 164 (2d Cir. 2025)：中国对 Hague 第 10 条已提反对，电邮送达无效 [56][57][58]。第五/第九巡回部分案件仍准 FRCP 4(f)(3) 电邮送达，存在分歧 [55]
- 执行：中美**无**相互承认执行判决条约，依《民诉法》§298/299（2023 修正）按互惠审查，§300(2)「未获合法传唤」可拒 [59]；上海金融法院 (2019)沪74协外认1号（2024-11-13）承认特拉华州衡平法院判决、上海高院 (2024)沪认复6号（2026-04-29）维持 [60]；温州中院 2025 年以「无互惠先例」拒认美国知识产权判决 [61] ⇒ 原告现实路径是压 Amazon / 保险 / 卖家账户资金，而非赴华执行

### 3.2 §25249.13 官方原文【核】

来源：<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=HSC&sectionNum=25249.13>（本轮 WebFetch 逐字取回）

> **25249.13. Preservation Of Existing Rights, Obligations, and Penalties.**
> "Nothing in this chapter shall alter or diminish any legal obligation otherwise required in common law or by statute or regulation, and **nothing in this chapter shall create or enlarge any defense in any action to enforce such legal obligation**. Penalties and sanctions imposed under this chapter shall be **in addition to** any penalties or sanctions otherwise prescribed by law."

⇒ 贴 Prop 65 警示**既不免除普通法侵权责任**（设计缺陷/未尽警示），**也不免除 FDA 义务**（by statute or regulation），且罚则叠加。这是「标了 warning 就安全」最直接的法定否定。

## 4. FDA 执行面【原】

### 8.4 保险与召回

- **Known-loss doctrine**【商/律所 23-d】：投保时已知或有理由知道损失具 "substantial probability"，风险即非偶然，保险人可拒赔/撤保（Outboard Marine v. Liberty Mutual 等）。
- **除外**：故意行为/欺诈、已知缺陷仍售、投保申请 misrepresentation（隐瞒品类、错误描述用途）、未披露配方/供应商/产地变更【商 25-d/23-d】；中国条款第 21、23 条同向【官 24-d】。
- **对本题**：投保时明知材料含铅 + 营销暗示食品用途，同时落入 known loss / intentional act / application misrepresentation，承保、理赔、续保三重风险；AIA 预审保司核验品类与用途，「错报用途」更难成立。
- **召回成本**【官 26-d，CPSC CPISA §222 报告】：库存与冲回销售、消费者通知、退货运费与销毁、额外仓储/人力、退款/维修/换货及回运、行政与诉讼、政府罚款；CPSC 估算平均直接成本 **$540,000**。第三方分项（1 万件量级）：通知 $40–80k、逆向物流 $150–250k、退款/换货 $300–600k、销毁 $25–50k、法律 $100–500k【商 27-d】。**产品责任险不赔召回费用**，须另购召回险；FBA 强制移除/退款追回叠加。
