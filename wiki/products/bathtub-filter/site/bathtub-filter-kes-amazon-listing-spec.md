# CH1 · Amazon Listing 规格（title / 五点 / A+ / 搜索词 / 合规）

## 这一页是什么

KES V1（游离氯版）Amazon listing 的**渠道规格页**：title 公式、五点 bullets 草案、A+ 模块规划、图片位、后台搜索词、Amazon 特有合规（claim policing / Q&A 预置 / 差评预防映射）。

**口径纪律**：所有 claim 措辞以 [claim-register](../bathtub-filter-claim-register.md)（§D 表面映射 + 广告行护栏）为执法源；EN 文案从 [website-copy-v1](../bathtub-filter-kes-website-copy-v1.md) 的 ship 口径改写，**不另造数字**；关键词从 [Amazon 类目与关键词基线](../bathtub-filter-amazon-category-and-keyword-baseline.md) 吸收；竞品话术边界参照 [11-ASIN 公开竞品证据](../bathtub-filter-11-asin-public-competitor-evidence-2026-06-02.md)。

**全站红线继承**（本页每节适用，不再逐条重复）：free chlorine 限定 / 成品未 NSF / 不软化 / 🔴 禁氯胺认领 / 🔴 禁 universal fit / 🔴 禁 eczema·baby-safe / PET 滤棉 + KDF55 130g + CaSO₃ 110g / 寿命 96/121 baths 口径引 [T3](./bathtub-filter-kes-page-replacement-and-lifespan.md)。

## 一、类目与 title

### 1.1 类目（沿 keyword-baseline §2，不重开决策）

**Tools & Home Improvement → Plumbing → Faucet Water Filters**。🔴 不走 Health & Household（触发 health-claim 高审查 + brand-gating，见 [claim-policing §1.2](../bathtub-filter-marketplace-claim-policing-layer.md)）；🔴 不走 Baby Products（CPSIA + 与 Canopy 直撞）。

### 1.2 Title 公式

```
[品牌 KES] + [品类词 Bath Tub Filter for Tub Faucet] + [free chlorine 限定]
+ [尺寸/适配词（有边界）] + [差异化词（透明滤仓/试纸）]
```

**草案（EN，最终定稿 `[____ 待市场]`）：**

> `KES Bath Tub Filter for Tub Faucet — Reduces Free Chlorine | Clear Cartridge, See the Real Media | Tool-Free Strap Install, Fits Standard US Tub Spouts (Check Fit Guide) | Free-Chlorine Test Strips Included`

规则（每条给理由，不是风格偏好）：

| 规则 | 为什么 |
|---|---|
| 必带 "free chlorine"，🔴 不写泛 "chlorine removal" / "total chlorine" | register Chlorine reduction 行：限定词非可选；泛氯措辞会被氯胺城市用户按字面买走 |
| 🔴 **禁 "chloramine" 关键词蹭量**（title/bullet/后台全域） | V1 对氯胺基本无效（register Banned V1 行）。氯胺城市用户搜到→买到→**"doesn't work as advertised" 差评**（negative-review-signals 最高杀伤簇 46.5%）。**错水型差评比没流量更贵**：流量缺口可以用 PPC 补，信任崩塌和 rating 掉档补不回来 |
| 🔴 **禁 "universal fit / fits all tubs / fits most tubs"** | register Banned；keyword-baseline §3.2 明确 "fits most tubs" 是结构性谎言；改用 "Fits Standard US Tub Spouts (Check Fit Guide)" 带边界指引 |
| 🔴 不带 "99%" 数字 | 无 NSF 成品认证下是 FTC substantiation 风险（baseline §3.1）；数字待 Gate 1 第三方 DPD |
| 🔴 不带 eczema / baby / skincare / softener / fluoride | register Banned + baseline §3.1/§3.2；baby 字样另触发 CPSIA 路径 |
| ⚠️ "sensitive skin" 仅可作 bullet 内 sensory framing（"designed with sensitive-skin bathers in mind"），不进 title 硬承诺 | register Conditional Skin-comfort 行 |

`[____ 待市场/运营]`：最终 title 定稿、价格、ASIN 结构（单品 / 变体：主机 vs 补芯 vs bundle——parent-child 结构建议接 [SVC9 Bundle 页](./bathtub-filter-kes-bundles-and-multipacks.md)，定价接 SVC7）。

## 二、五点 bullets 草案（EN）

从 [website-copy-v1](../bathtub-filter-kes-website-copy-v1.md) PDP 口径改写；**每点标 claim 出处行**，ship 前逐条过 register。

**B1 · 干什么（精度限定）**
> `REDUCES FREE CHLORINE AT BATH-FILL — Built for city tap water disinfected with free chlorine. Strongest in the fresh-filter, best-experience segment at 15 L/min fill flow. Independently tested reduction figure: [____ 去氯率·待第三方 DPD 实测]. Not for chloramine cities — check your city's water first.`
> *出处：register Allowed「Chlorine reduction」行（free chlorine + fresh-filter + 15 L/min 限定词不可删）；数字占位规则照 website-copy 头注；「check your city first」= 诚实劝退 Allowed 行。*

**B2 · 看得见的料（透明差异化）**
> `SEE THE REAL MEDIA — A clear cartridge: polyester (PET) fiber disc → KDF55 copper-zinc (130 g) → calcium sulfite (110 g), layered never mixed, with an internal flow-diversion module. Each layer replaceable separately. No filler hiding behind opaque plastic.`
> *出处：register Allowed「Media transparency」行 + Conditional「Head-to-head with mixed-media」行（讲设计选择、不点名竞品）；克数照 V1 BOM 裁定。*

**B3 · 自己验证（试纸机制）**
> `TEST IT YOURSELF — Every filter ships with free-chlorine test strips. Compare your tap water before and after the filter. Do not use a TDS pen — this product does not target TDS reduction.`
> *出处：register Allowed「Verification-by-user」行原文改写；🔴 TDS 笔禁作验证（Banned §C）。*

**B4 · 有边界的适配**
> `TOOL-FREE, BOUNDED FIT — Adjustable 21 mm silicone strap fits standard US tub spouts. Three install routes cover pull-diverter, straight, and curved spouts. Designed for typical US fill rates (18–25 L/min); slow the fill if it splashes. Not for loose/wobbly spouts or wide waterfall spouts — see the fit guide before you buy.`
> *出处：register Allowed「Fit / compatibility」行（每条兼容必带「不支持」边界）+ [P4 安装指南](./bathtub-filter-kes-install-and-compatibility-guide.md) S-05/S-06 不支持行。*

**B5 · 更换口径（baths 不用月）**
> `REPLACE WHEN THE STRIP SAYS SO — Test your after-filter water with the included free-chlorine strips. Replace when the strip starts showing chlorine again. Lifespan depends on your tap chlorine and how often you bathe — the typical range is printed in the manual.`
> *出处：register Allowed「Replacement-trigger」行；寿命数字口径唯一来源 [T3](./bathtub-filter-kes-page-replacement-and-lifespan.md)，本页不另造。*

> ⚠️ 五点全域自检（2026-09-05 更新）：B4 去掉 35 L/min（非 V1 配置数据）；B5 去掉 96 / 121 baths（内部模型 🟡，Amazon 表面不承载），改为试纸可执行触发，数字留说明书与 PDP 网页版。无 chloramine / universal / eczema / baby-safe / softener / 99% / clinically；「(~9–10 months)」换算参照**不进 bullets**（Amazon 表面从严，只用 baths——月份换算留给 PDP 网页版 T3 的带限定语境）。

## 三、A+ content 模块规划

对应 site 页做单一真理源引用，**A+ 不新造口径**。⚠️ claim-policing §1.3：A+ **图片里的文字同样被审**——高风险词不得藏图。

| A+ 模块 | 内容 | 口径源（site 页） |
|---|---|---|
| **M-A1 · Look Inside（横幅解剖）** | 透明滤仓实拍 + 三层标注（PET fiber → KDF55 130g → CaSO₃ 110g）+ 导流模块示意；文案 = website-copy「What's inside」 | [M1 CaSO₃](./bathtub-filter-kes-media-calcium-sulfite.md) / [M2 KDF55](./bathtub-filter-kes-media-kdf55-copper-zinc.md) / [M7 滤棉](./bathtub-filter-kes-media-pp-cotton.md)；[D3 透明滤仓](./bathtub-filter-kes-structure-transparent-housing.md) |
| **M-A2 · How to verify（试纸对比）** | 试纸 before/after 三步图；"not a TDS pen" 句必在；NSF 口径若出现必须完整料级句 + "finished product is not itself NSF-certified" | [T1 自测诊断](./bathtub-filter-kes-page-water-test-diagnosis.md) + [T2 我们怎么测](./bathtub-filter-kes-page-how-we-test-and-certify.md) |
| **M-A3 · 适配边界（Will it fit?）** | 三条安装路线各一图 + 支持/不支持速查（含 🔴 明示不支持：松动龙头 / 瀑布宽体嘴）；引导 "check the fit guide" | [P4 安装与兼容](./bathtub-filter-kes-install-and-compatibility-guide.md) S-01~S-08 |
| **M-A4 · 防买错分流（Which water?）** | P5 §四 水型速查表复用（free chlorine ✅ / chloramine ❌ not yet / well ⚠️ / hard water ⚠️ 阻垢不软化）；「It does not soften your water.」随行 | [P5 包装 spec §四](./bathtub-filter-kes-packaging-design-spec.md) / [T1](./bathtub-filter-kes-page-water-test-diagnosis.md) |
| **M-A5 · 维护与寿命** | 排水晾干三步 + 更换触发（baths 口径）；防霉操作闭环 | [P3 维护指南](./bathtub-filter-kes-care-and-maintenance-guide.md) + [T3](./bathtub-filter-kes-page-replacement-and-lifespan.md) |

🔴 A+ 全域禁：before/after **皮肤**对比图（触发额外审查，claim-policing §1.2/§9.3）；"clinically proven"；认证 logo（无 certifier 证书上传即拒）；脏水/毒水恐吓视觉（register Banned toxin-panic）。试纸的 before/after 是**水的读数**对比，允许——但图注只讲 ppm 读数，不讲皮肤结果。

## 六、Amazon 特有合规

### 6.1 Claim policing 对策（引 [marketplace-claim-policing-layer](../bathtub-filter-marketplace-claim-policing-layer.md)）

- **按平台标准写而非仅 FTC 标准**：Amazon 拦截比 FTC 早且频繁（policing 页一句话结论）。
- **substantiation 文件包随时可上传**（policing §9.2）：每条 claim 备好出具方 / exact scope / 测试条件 / 报告原件 / 有效期——NSF/ANSI 42 料级 listing（C0843384-01）、TÜV SÜD 721682290C、Gate 1 DPD 报告（出来后）。⚠️ 宗立卫生报告**禁用于广告**（register §B 注），只入内部存档不上传作功效背书。
- **Prop 65 字段**：Seller Central 合规字段正确填写（否则加州买家不可下单）；措辞 `[____ 待法务]`，与 P5 §三 #5 同源。
- **类目防漂移**：不因运营冲量改挂 Health & Household；改类目 = 换审查制度。
- **takedown 预案**：被 report 后申诉靠文件包，不靠改口——所以第一天就不写申诉不了的话。

### 6.2 Q&A 预置答案（氯胺 / 软化 / 婴儿三连必答，照 [website-copy PDP FAQ](../bathtub-filter-kes-website-copy-v1.md) 口径逐字改写）

- **Q: Does it remove chloramine?**
  > `Not this version. If your city uses chloramine, this free-chlorine filter won't help — we'd rather tell you before you buy. Check your city's water report (CCR) or our fit guide first.`
- **Q: Does it soften hard water?**
  > `No. It reduces free chlorine. It does not soften your water. The optional anti-scale module helps protect your tub and fixtures from scale buildup — but that protects fixtures, it doesn't soften water either.`
- **Q: Is it safe for babies / good for eczema?**
  > `We don't make health or skin claims. Many families use a bath filter for chlorine-sensitive bathtime routines — but we won't tell you it treats anything.`
- 追加预置（按 SVC1 FAQ 口径）：**Does it remove heavy metals?**（不作健康声称）/ **How do I know it's working?**（试纸前后对比，not a TDS pen）/ **Will it fit my faucet?**（指向 fit guide + 不支持清单）。

### 6.3 差评预防映射（[negative-review-signals](../bathtub-filter-marketplace-negative-review-signals.md) 逐条 → listing 内容对策）

| 高杀伤投诉（占 1-2 星） | listing 内容对策 |
|---|---|
| 整体无效 / "doesn't work as advertised"（46.5%） | B1 只认领 free chlorine + 限定词全带；M-A4 防买错分流把氯胺/井水/RO 用户在购买前劝走；B3/M-A2 给试纸客观验证路径（体感不明显 ≠ 无效） |
| 质量破损 / 做工（19.5%） | FFP 简装规格照 P5 §六 #4（跌落测试 `[____ 待供应链]`）；图 6 套装实拍与 P2 清单一致，杜绝「到货少件/与图不符」 |
| 绕流 / 溢流 / 过滤路径失效（19.0%） | B4 + M-A3 写明设计流量（18–25 L/min 典型）与放慢注水指引（35 L/min 数字待 V1 复测，不上 listing）；图 3 展示正确水路；不支持类型明示 |
| 敏感肌 / 宝宝场景效果不稳（18.5%） | 全 listing 零 baby/eczema 承诺——sensory framing 封顶；期望管理写在 claim 而非售后 |
| 宣传口径争议（重金属 / TDS 等，17.3%） | 不列 broad contaminant stack；B3 主动写 "does not target TDS"；重金属只答 Q&A 且不作健康声称 |
| 发霉 / 卫生（13.6%） | M-A5 排水晾干闭环上 A+；随盒防霉护理卡（P5 §六 #3）形成图文双触达 |
| slips off / 适配失败（cluster B） | B4 边界 + 图 5 不支持速查——让不适配的人**买前**自我排除；🔴 不写 fits most |
